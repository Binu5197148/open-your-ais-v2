## X, ingles

```
In one of the repos I read this week, the best page is a table of failures.

The skill turns a picture of a robotic animal into a 3D object that takes itself apart on camera. The table lists what went wrong along the way.

A moth graded itself dense enough. It came out as a clean white diagram and was thrown out after 9 rounds.

A piranha passed on density in round 2. It looked like a crocodile: jaws at 0.52 of the body length, against 0.37 in the picture.

A flying octopus had wings and tentacles fighting for the same half second.

A scarab took 4 rounds and about 450,000 tokens and still looked cheap.

Four animals were built at once and reviewed after round 6. One moth alone used about 430,000 tokens before it was dropped.

Every row ends with the line it added to the brief. Bristles got a ratio. Density gets measured. Two animals at a time, reviewed after round 2.

It is one of five skills in this week's 5 Week Skills. I read each one through the notes it keeps about itself.
```
